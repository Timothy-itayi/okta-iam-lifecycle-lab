# Leave hub — 10. The week and the flag

Date: 2026-10-08, 05:48–05:54 Sydney. The decision page shows who else is off or rostered that week, and a manager or HR can say what Jev should have suggested. Neither one decides the request.

## Team that week

The grid is Monday to Friday of the week the request starts in. A request that runs into the next week gets a second grid. Each row is an active person in that department. Terminated staff are left out.

A day is hatched when that person has open leave covering it, gray when the leave is approved, and light blue when they are rostered and not away. The cell name is the person, the weekday, and that state.

## Flag

"Flag Jev's suggestion" opens the form on the decision page. The choices are approve, deny, and needs review, plus a note. Save writes a `jev_flags` row. The request status does not change, and Jev's stored recommendation does not change. The staff request page does not show the flag. A person cannot flag their own request.

The decision column stays in view while the week grid is on screen.
